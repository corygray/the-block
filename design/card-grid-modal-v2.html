Design Review — v2 Card Grid selected. Now swapping the detail view from an inline expand to a popover modal — accessible by construction (native <dialog>, focus trap, Escape-to-close, focus returns to the card on close).
Why a modal here, and not on the row-based directions: a "quick view" popover over a card grid is a well-established pattern (Amazon/Etsy-style), unlike over a dense table row. The tradeoff is real — the grid behind it is inert while open — but that's inherent to any true modal, and it's a more expected tradeoff for a card grid than it would be for a dense list.
  • Opens with <dialog>.showModal() — focus trap and Escape-to-close come from the browser, not hand-rolled JS
  • Focus moves to the dialog's heading on open (title announced first), and back to the exact card that opened it on close
  • Clicking the dimmed backdrop closes it; so does the close button or Escape
  • Filter sidebar and the Sort toolbar are unchanged from v1 — the exploration was always about the list item, not about removing filtering/sorting
